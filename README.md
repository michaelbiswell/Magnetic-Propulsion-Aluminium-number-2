1. The Material Science Blueprint (The Layers)
Reviewers will immediately look for structural failure points caused by the heat and magnetic pressure. We mitigate this by breaking the long, thin barrel down into three specific material layers:
•	Layer 1: The Plasma-Facing Liner (Tungsten-Rhenium Alloy - W-Re):
o	Why: Pure tungsten handles extreme heat but is brittle. Adding Rhenium drastically increases the metal’s ductility, thermal shock resistance, and electrical conductivity.
o	Function: This thin, internal liner serves as the ultra-smooth, conductive track that directly carries the Lorentz current alongside the accelerating aluminium plasma.
•	Layer 2: The Insulating Substrate (Aluminium Nitride - AlN, or Beryllia - BeO):
o	Why: This layer must isolate massive voltages between the tracks while acting as a thermal highway to dump heat out of the core. Aluminium Nitride features exceptionally high thermal conductivity while remaining a completely unreactive electrical insulator.
o	Function: It sandwiches the internal liner, ensuring the plasma's electrical arc doesn't short-circuit into the rest of the spacecraft frame.
•	Layer 3: The Structural Support Sleeve (Carbon-Carbon Matrix Composite):
o	Why: To keep the overall engine weight low, we swap heavy titanium armor for space-grade carbon fiber woven under immense pressure.
o	Function: This lightweight jacket completely encases the barrel. It holds the long, thin tube together against the outward explosive forces generated by the internal magnetic pressures (P_B).
